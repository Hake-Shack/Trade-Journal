THE HAKE SHACK · JOURNAL SETTINGS

Settings & Backup

Keep your journal safe. Backups are the single most important maintenance task — data lives only in this browser, on this device.

Appearance

Choose the theme that feels best for long review sessions. Both themes use the same colours — only the surfaces change.

Theme
Dark is the default. Light uses a warm off-white background and keeps the same accent palette.

Backup & Restore

Your trades, reviews, trophies and screenshots live only in this browser. A full backup saves everything to a single file you can store in your cloud drive or email to yourself. Do this at least once a month.

Export full backup
Downloads a single .json file containing every trade, screenshot, review and trophy. Keep it somewhere safe. You can restore from this file on any device.
Import backup
Restore from a previously exported .json file. Choose Merge to add the backup on top of your current data, or Replace to wipe and restore exactly.

Data at a glance

A quick summary of what's stored right now. If any number surprises you, take a backup.

Danger zone

These actions are permanent. Type the confirmation word to proceed. Export a backup first.

Clear all trades
Removes every trade and its screenshot. Reviews and trophies are kept.
Clear all reviews
Removes every weekly review. Trades and trophies are kept.
Clear trophies
Removes all earned challenge badges. Trades and reviews are kept.
Reset everything
Deletes all trades, screenshots, reviews and trophies. The journal returns to a blank state. This cannot be undone.
